✦ About NOVA AI

Built for speed,
designed for clarity

NOVA is a beautiful, privacy-first AI assistant that runs entirely in your browser. No accounts, no tracking — just pure intelligence.

⚡ Why Groq?

Groq's LPU™ (Language Processing Unit) is purpose-built for AI inference, delivering speeds 10x faster than GPU-based alternatives. NOVA uses Groq exclusively for the fastest possible responses.

🔒 Privacy First

Your conversations are stored only in your browser's localStorage. Nothing is ever sent to any server except the AI provider you choose.

🧠 Multiple Models

NOVA supports the latest open-weight reasoning models from OpenAI, Alibaba, and more.

🎨 Design Philosophy

Every pixel of NOVA is crafted for focus and delight. Cinematic gradients, smooth animations, and a dark-first aesthetic make long sessions a pleasure.
